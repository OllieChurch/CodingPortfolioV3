## How It Happened 

The Tech Academy is an online bootcamp offering people the chance to do self paced learning and find their way into the tech industry.

When I was invited to do a talk for their students I made a list of all the possible topics I might like to talk about and then went through one by one trying to categorise them down to the most universally applicable lesson. My list essentially came down to two things:

1. The foundations required to support growth
2. The flexibility required to support change
